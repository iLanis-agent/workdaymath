Workday Math

Twelve calendar days are not twelve working days.

Boundary: arithmetic only Workday Math counts weekdays between the dates you type and skips only the holidays you list. It does not know any country's or company's holidays, and it does not say whether a deadline is realistic or what a contract allows. You decide that.

The count truth

From 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-30 inclusive: 12 calendar days, 2 weekend days, 1 holiday, 9 working days. Both ends are counted.

The add truth

5 working days after 2026-10-19: 2026-10-27. The start day is not counted, and the listed holiday is skipped.

The buffer truth

Working days after 2026-10-19 through 2026-10-30 inclusive: 8. Needed: 6. Spare: 2. When the job needs more, it says how many days short.

Run your numbers
Workday Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
