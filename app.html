Workday Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts weekdays between the dates you type and skips only the holidays you list. It does not know any country's or company's holidays, and it does not say whether a deadline is realistic or what a contract allows.

Your holidays

Dates you list, written like 2026-10-26 and separated by commas. Weekends are always skipped. Leave it empty for none.

The count truth

Every kind of day between two dates, both ends included.

The add truth

The date a number of working days after a start date. The start day is not counted.

The buffer truth

Working days after the start through the deadline, against the working days a job needs.

Workday Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
